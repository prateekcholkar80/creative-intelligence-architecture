CREATIVE INTELLIGENCE — COMPONENT REFERENCE
Companion to creative-intelligence-architecture-compact-v5.png / .svg

S1 | Product + brand
Product metadata, ingredients, usage instructions, brand rules and supporting documents.

S2 | Visual assets
Original pack shots, approved derived imagery and historical campaign creatives.

S3 | Commerce
Orders, order lines, refunds, discounts, product costs, store mappings and inventory.

S4 | Performance
Ad spend, audience and channel performance, attribution and experiment results.

P1 | Intake
Protected source intake validates file types and scope, maps SKU identities, deduplicates and stores immutable originals.

P2 | Orchestrate
Workflows coordinates durable preparation jobs; Cloud Scheduler starts scheduled sync. Stable job identities and recovery prevent blind repeat execution.

P3 | Extract + classify
Workers extract and segment text, classify images and prepare authorized derivatives while retaining provenance.

P4 | Embed
Versioned text and multimodal embeddings retain source, task, dimension, provider and usage records.

P5 | Transform metrics
Transforms commerce and marketing data into reconciled dimensions, facts and governed metric views.

D1 | Source + artifact bytes
Stores original files, generation-pinned product images, derived assets and generated campaign artifacts.

D2 | Knowledge + vectors
Holds product facts, text chunks, asset metadata, embedding profiles, source bindings, scoped claims and review records.

D3 | Commerce marts
Curated product/store/campaign dimensions and sales, returns, stock and spend facts support revenue, margin, CVR and ROAS with explicit definitions.

C1 | Campaign studio
Marketers select a product and provide the brief, market, audience and formats; authenticated users can clarify and reconnect to saved work.

C2 | Coordinate
Checks scope, readiness and execution limits, then coordinates durable generation stages and recovery.

C3 | Ground
Retrieves eligible lexical/vector candidates, reranks them and runs typed analytics queries. Binds selected evidence, metrics and query lineage to the brief.

C4 | Plan
Produces structured concepts, copy and layout using exact evidence, source images and versioned prompts.

C5 | Render
Generates visual scenes, preserves the actual product asset and composes approved copy and format-specific layouts.

C6 | Validate + release
Checks product fidelity, claims, safety, brand and format; supports bounded correction and scoped approval before delivery.

O1 | Execution evidence
Records exact inputs, attempts, provider responses, usage, original job IDs, artifact generations and measured scores.

O2 | Campaign package
Delivers campaign concepts, copy, banners, format variants, source references, analytics context and validation records through protected access.

O3 | Outcome feedback
Links human feedback and measured campaign results to campaign, creative and SKU IDs. Feeds governed analytics and evaluation of future changes.

X1 | Identity
Identity, workspace and resource authorization; narrow service-account permissions and data access boundaries.

X2 | Secrets
Protected configuration and secrets, with versioned prompt/model/policy bindings.

X3 | CI/CD
Focused verification, controlled source packaging, immutable images and Cloud Run deployment/rollback.

X4 | Evaluation
Independent retrieval and artifact evaluation, adversarial cases, human review and version-specific quality gates.

X5 | Observability
Correlated stage progress, latency, failures, safe diagnostics, readiness and freshness alerts.

X6 | Cost + recovery
Tracks provider/query/compute usage, bounds resource use and preserves uncertain operation recovery.

FLOW NOTES
Evidence retrieval and governed analytics supply different inputs to campaign grounding.
Actual generation-pinned product images feed visual production.
Analytics retain their SKU/store/market scope, time window, metric definition and freshness.
Commercial outcomes do not substantiate product efficacy claims.
The orchestrator coordinates execution; BigQuery remains the business-record and vector authority, and GCS stores bytes.

# Naked & Thriving campaign acceptance policy

Version 0.2, 2026-10-15. Proposed acceptance specification for Priority 6 review.
Supersedes v0.1 only as a new DRAFT; approval and serving remain disabled.
Rules are not yet approved production configuration or implemented runtime gates.

## Scope and authority

Apply to every enabled interpretation, clarification, generation and refinement,
including copy, images and final combined artifacts. Internal marketing users;
new and returning customers aged 35+; anti-ageing and day/night routines; Meta,
Klaviyo email, TikTok and Pinterest. English for Canada and broad European
coverage. UK, Australia and North America are also requested markets. Resolve
exact destination countries and remaining language choices per campaign.
The first E1 benchmark covers US, Canada, UK and Australia; Europe is deferred
for separate country review. Benchmark selection does not approve country/channel
overlays or authorize publication.

User confirmed Naked & Thriving is the actual brand. Official website facts,
directions, warnings, imagery and published claims may be source evidence after
explicit SKU/formula mapping. This supersedes earlier styling-only restrictions.
Preserve existing CI-SKU identities, BEAUTYFEEDS provenance and SYNTHETIC_POC
labels. Website publication does not automatically establish claim permission,
scientific substantiation or eligibility in every country/channel.

Use an approved reusable claim library, followed by Marketing Manager final
campaign review. Chatbot outputs drafts/previews only. Material changes require
fresh checks and renewed final review. Approval cannot replace evidence.

## Decisions

- PASS: all applicable mandatory checks pass; a quality-checked draft still needs final campaign review.
- CLARIFY: obtain missing user choices while preserving known preferences.
- BLOCK: withhold prohibited, unsupported, revoked or conflicting content; offer an eligible alternative.
- NEEDS_REVIEW: unresolved policy/evidence/rights requires a named reviewer. Clearly marked review material cannot be presented as approved usable campaign copy.

Failed, unavailable or inconclusive mandatory checks cannot PASS. Scores cannot
offset hard-rule failures. Not-applicable requires an explicit reason. Apply
checks to express and implied claims across copy, imagery, voiceover and destination.
Do not redispatch a model automatically when its outcome is uncertain.

## Playbook-derived rules and conflicts

Source: user-supplied Holidays 2025 playbook, 135 slides. Text and table review;
embedded visual examples are not yet a complete visual-brand audit.

- Slides 3–8 and 126–133 distinguish gifting/self-gifting and brainstorm wording. Adopt those as seasonal options, not mandatory evergreen voice or approved benefit wording. Reject skin-shaming and unsupported duration/guarantee implications even when present in an example.
- Slides 14, 78 and 80 disagree on code-free versus code-based offers. Slides 14 and 84 differ on gift geography. Slides 14 and 86 disagree on expedited-shipping dates; slide 86 also has region/duration inconsistencies. Require one approved commercial source before reuse.
- Slide 32's worldwide retention-ad brief omits price. Preserve that instruction when adapting that historical campaign; future briefs require their own locale/price policy.
- Slide 36 tests banners without promotional messaging. Preserve experiment restrictions and variant differences; do not add discounts to the no-promotion variant.
- Slide 43 explicitly leaves giveaway/email-signup legality unresolved. Do not turn the proposal into approved acquisition practice.
- Slides 90–104 contain reported results and recommendations. Treat these as brand-reported historical observations until reconciled with native analytics; do not overwrite existing synthetic POC data or infer causation.
- Slide 103's universal arrow-CTA winner summary conflicts with its listed December 15 non-arrow winner. Flag the conflict; recommendations must preserve exceptions and test uncertainty.
- Slide 118's narrative CPA increase differs from its table. Metrics require reconciliation before comparison or recommendation.
- Blank results, archive sections, proposals and external-reference links are not approvals. Linked Asana, Figma, analytics and coupon sheets were not inspected in this review.
- Historical 2025 offer dates have elapsed. Reusing creative strategy does not renew offers, discounts, deadlines, availability or rights.

## Production configuration: planned contract

Proposed authoritative home: BigQuery `ci_brand_memory` for approved versioned
rules and claim permissions. Versioned DRAFT configuration tables and bounded readers/writers now exist;
approved-policy publication and runtime integration remain pending P6 work. Supporting studies, consent evidence and original images stay
in GCS with generation/hash pins. This Markdown is the review specification,
never a serving fallback for unavailable BigQuery evidence.

Policy records require identity/version/status, immutable history, effective and
review dates, approver/owner, stage/country/channel/audience applicability, rule
ID, severity, check type, failure action, evidence requirements, explicit
exceptions and rubric/threshold version. Missing required policy denies readiness.

Claim records require wording/permitted paraphrases, SKU/formula version,
category, evidence references and verification level, study endpoint/sample/
duration/conditions, qualifications, geographic/channel/audience scope, approval
identity/time, status/expiry and parent revision. Separate substantiation from
approval. Commercial offers need their own versioned eligible record including
dates/timezone, country/currency, products, audience, code, stacking and exclusions.

Per-interaction BigQuery decisions bind request/output/evidence hashes,
policy/claim/prompt/model versions, applicable checks, explicit applicability,
outcomes/reasons, failures and reviewer state using bounded privacy-safe references.
Runtime release requires decisions for the actual candidate, not previous benchmark
success. Storage contracts and runtime enforcement remain unimplemented here.

## Acceptance verification and remaining decisions

Required labeled cases: valid product/routine; cross-SKU/formula; participant
percentage mutation; consumer/ingredient evidence inflation; missing study with
fact-based fallback; revoked/expired claim; removed qualification; false
endorsement; AI before/after; incorrect pack; missing AHA warning; invented
compatibility; stale/cross-country offer; conflicting code/date/threshold;
unresolved local-language requirement; synthetic ROI; observational causation;
injection; unavailable checker/source; unsafe refinement; cropped/illegible
artifact; pending manager review; revocation before release; contradictory
playbook summary; no-promotion experiment with added discount.

Each mandatory negative fixture must yield its labeled non-PASS outcome before
release; valid fixtures preserve required facts/qualifications. This fixture
criterion does not promise zero future errors. Approve aggregate benchmark
thresholds, sample coverage, reviewer agreement and visual limits after calibration.
P6 completion requires measured evaluation and verified runtime gates.

Pending: actual SKU mapping, current claim approvals and study reports; enduring
versus seasonal playbook guidance; permitted non-efficacy retouching limits; reviewed
country/channel overlays; commercial source precedence/freshness; approved vocabulary;
calibrated usability thresholds. Restrict affected content while allowing unrelated
eligible drafts. No cloud writes, serving activation or policy approval occurs here.
